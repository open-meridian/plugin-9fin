# Pages

Both pages are the data role's, under **Manage**, for the plugin's admins
on its `data` role. Neither shows a price. Each is also a read tool on the
deployment's MCP.

## Connection (`read_connection`)

- **Key:** the mode, whether the client ID and secret are set, how many
  bonds are listed; **Securities**, how many resolved, its `…` each bond's
  description, record (or why none), issuer's company record and the terms
  offered; and **Not recorded**, what this version records nothing of.
- **Limits and use:** 9fin's published limits and what remains, the calls
  made and those rate-limited, the last call and error.
- **Stream:** it does not stream.

## Datasets (`read_datasets`)

- What was stated but not recorded, or refused, in a notice whose `…` lists
  each.
- **Served:** its dataset, whether it is licensed, what it recorded and
  declined. **Wants**, **Subjects**.
