# Terms

## Whose terms

The terms under which you use 9fin's data are between you and 9fin, in your
agreement or order form with it. The dataset describes these terms in words
on the Data sources page; the plugin declares no licence: the deployment
admin sets the dataset's licence there.
What follows describes 9fin's published terms in plain words, as read on 30
October 2026 (9fin's terms of service v1.0, effective 12 May 2026, and its
data services trial terms), to help you and your agent decide; it is not
legal advice, and 9fin's own text governs.

## 9fin's published terms

- **Licence**: non-exclusive, non-transferable, personal and not
  sub-licensable, for authorised users at an authorised team, location or
  site.
- **Internal use**: small portions of the data for internal or interoffice
  use; no dissemination to third parties without 9fin's consent, except 9fin
  data clearly marked as 9fin's in client materials, with its disclaimers;
  third parties' data excluded from that.
- **No derivative works** beyond that, and **no AI training**: the data may
  not be used to develop, build, fine-tune or train models.
- **Automated access**: the terms of service limit requests to what a person
  could reasonably make; an API order form sets the API's own limits.
- **Termination**: 9fin may ask that its content be destroyed.
- **Trials** are for internal evaluation only, their data deleted within 30
  days.
- **Pricing** is not published.

## Underlying sources

9fin's own analysts and journalists for its instrument data; its ratings at
issuance are the rating agencies' (not recorded here).

## Open questions

Put to 9fin: which token address is current (its OpenAPI files and its
authentication guide name different ones); whether price talk is available
by stage, as structured fields; what a deployment may keep and display.
